# AppLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-16

This privacy policy explains how AppLock (the "App") handles information when you use it on your Android device.

## 1. Overview

AppLock is a local device security app that helps users protect selected apps with authentication methods such as PIN, biometric unlock, and related lock-screen protections.

We designed AppLock to work primarily on-device. The app does not require an account, does not use ads, and does not use analytics or tracking services.

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit the following to our servers:

- Full contact lists
- SMS, call logs, or message content
- Photos or videos from other apps
- Browser history
- GPS or precise location
- Contacts, email addresses, or personal identifiers from your device
- Account information
- Advertising identifiers or marketing data

## 3. Information processed locally on your device

The App may process limited information locally on your device to provide its core functionality, including:

- The list of apps you choose to lock
- Your lock preferences and settings
- Your PIN or biometric state for local app protection
- Optional intruder selfie or security event information, if you enable that feature

This information is stored on your device and is used only to provide the app's locking and protection features. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties.

## 4. Permissions and why they are used

The App may request Android permissions to support its security features. These permissions are used only for the functions described below:

### Accessibility Service

The App may use Android Accessibility Service to detect when a protected app is opened and to display a lock screen or challenge before access is allowed.

This permission is used for app protection and device security. It does not read or transmit your personal content, messages, or passwords. It only detects app foreground activity needed to enforce the lock state.

### Device Admin

The App may use device admin features to help prevent unauthorized uninstallation while the app is active and to support device protection workflows. This is used only to protect the app and its lock state. The App does not control your device beyond the requested protection features.

### Camera

The App may access the camera if the user enables intruder detection or related security photo features. Camera access is used only for local device-side capture of a security event if enabled by the user. These images are not uploaded to any server operated by us unless the user chooses to manually export or share them.

### Notification permission

The App may request notification permission to show relevant security alerts and reminders.

### Internet access

Internet access may be used only for the optional in-app browser feature or for direct access to websites the user chooses to visit. The App does not use internet access for advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

## 5. Data storage and security

The App stores security settings and related local data directly on your device. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect local app data. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Third-party services

AppLock does not use advertising networks, analytics providers, social media SDKs, or account-based tracking services. We do not share personal data with third parties for marketing or profiling.

If you choose to use an optional browser feature or navigate to a website outside the App, that website will be governed by the website owner's privacy policy and terms of use.

## 7. Children’s privacy

AppLock is not directed to children under the age of 13 and is not intended for use by children without parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Your choices

You may disable or remove permissions in your device settings at any time, subject to the app's functionality. You may also uninstall the App at any time.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised policy in the App or on the App listing where applicable.
