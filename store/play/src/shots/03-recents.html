Hide locked apps from recents
The app switcher has no picture of it to show. It reopens at its main screen after you unlock.
Close them again when you leave
The screen goes black for a moment when you leave a locked app, while AppLock closes it.
Uninstall protection
On — AppLock cannot be uninstalled. Tap to turn off.
Lock Settings and the installer
Ask for your PIN before Settings, the app installer or your phone's security app can be opened
Uninstall protection can still be switched off from Settings > Security > Device admin apps. Turn this on to put the PIN in front of that too.
